        The field

        How to run the plan

        This map assumes a lower-left start. R1 to R6 cover the six Oil Refineries. The Hospital and Military pairs stage near their objectives and wait for them to open.

        Floaters reinforce as called, then stage near the Rare Earth Factory before each alloy spawn. Floater 1 is the primary carrier, Floater 2 is backup, and Floaters 3 and 4 protect the active carrier back to HQ.

        CP is the generator's only input for strength. The caller can adjust for march speed, survivability and attendance before sharing.

        Guides: RDRZ, Z-War, Packsify (each opens in a new tab).
